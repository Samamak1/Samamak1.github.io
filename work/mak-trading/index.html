November 2019 to present

Built and operated a paid education community.

The visible product was market education. The operating work behind it covered acquisition, onboarding, curriculum, recurring content, member support, moderation, retention, payments, access control, and a platform migration.

The problem

A growing community could not be run through content alone. New members needed a clear path into the program, paid access had to match billing status, support questions needed owners, and content had to be delivered on a reliable cadence. The original LaunchPass and Stripe setup also created limits as the program grew.

My responsibility

I led the member lifecycle and business operation. I worked across brand, acquisition, onboarding, curriculum, content, moderation, support, retention, payments, and platform delivery. External engineers supported the technical implementation.

What I did

  • Built the original 48-page curriculum and organized the member learning path.
  • Created recurring content, onboarding, support, moderation, and retention routines.
  • Tracked subscription, payment, trial, and member-access issues across Stripe and the community platform.
  • Coordinated the migration from LaunchPass and Stripe to WordPress and MemberPress while preserving member access and communication.
  • Directed external engineering work and made product decisions around the real member workflow.

Three decisions that shaped the program

Build the lifecycle, not only the audience

Acquisition mattered, but onboarding, support, content rhythm, retention, and payment access determined whether the community could operate at scale.

Move off the first platform before it became a larger constraint

The migration created more control over content, billing, and access, even though it required careful continuity planning.

Use financial evidence carefully

Public figures are tied to dated Stripe screenshots. They describe the documented state at that time and do not claim current revenue.

Result

The community reached approximately 2,000 members across channels and more than 1,200 cumulative paid subscribers. A Stripe snapshot documented $28,696 in monthly recurring revenue and 321 active subscriptions at one point in the paid program.
