How I work

I learned systems in engineering and judgment in operations

Engineering taught me to start with the load, the constraint, and the evidence. Operations taught me what happens when a good plan meets a busy floor, a fixed deadline, and people who need clear instructions.

I usually enter the work when information is scattered and the rules live in different places. I gather the source material, write down what cannot be ignored, build a process another person can follow, and check the result against what happened. That approach has carried from building design into restaurant openings, electronics testing, product requirements, trading research, and AI quality review.
