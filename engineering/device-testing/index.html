Intake, testing, grading, and disposition procedures for more than 400 retired devices, with custody records a client can audit and data disposition aligned to NIST 800-88.

The work

Every device that comes through the door gets the same treatment. Intake against the pickup manifest, a power-on test, BIOS-level checks, a grade, and a disposition, reuse, resale, or recycling. Each step writes a record, so a client can ask later what arrived, what was tested, how the data was handled, and where each asset went, and get a documented answer instead of a memory.

For a healthcare pilot client I delivered a verified 130-unit test log with device-level records and chain of custody documentation from intake to disposition. I also wrote the data destruction and handling procedures, aligned to NIST 800-88, with SHA-256 certificate verification on the wipe records.

The records

The platform screens below show how the test and custody records are organized. Batch details carry the device list with model, serial, grade, weight, and wipe certificate status. The chain of custody view is a timestamped timeline from pickup request through intake, with the technician, the container seals, and the manifest attached at each step. The evidence view holds the photos and documents that back each record.

The platform was built by a development vendor to requirements I wrote from the field work. Screens show fictional sample data.
