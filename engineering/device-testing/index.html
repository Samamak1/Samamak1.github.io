Device testing and chain of custody at RYGNeco

At RYGNeco I turned device pickup, intake, testing, grading, data handling, and disposition into one traceable record. A healthcare pilot produced verified records for 130 devices.

Where
RYGNeco, Cincinnati
When
August 2024 to present
Role
Field procedures, records, and product requirements
Scope
Testing, custody, evidence, and reporting
130
Devices in the verified healthcare pilot record

Each device was matched to its pickup record, powered on, checked at the BIOS level, graded, and assigned to reuse, resale, or recycling. The same record followed the asset through custody and disposition, showing what arrived, what worked, how data was handled, and where the device went.

For the healthcare pilot, I delivered device level test results and chain of custody records for 130 devices. I used what we learned in the field to define product requirements for accounts, pickups, inventory, evidence, certificates, finance, and client reporting.

I used Google Sheets and Google Docs for operating records and procedures, Outlook for customer and vendor communication, and ChatGPT and Claude to structure requirements. Cursor and GitHub kept the product work organized as those requirements moved to the development vendor.
