The work

Every device follows the same path. It is checked against the pickup record, powered on, reviewed at the BIOS level, graded, and assigned to reuse, resale, or recycling. Each step adds to the same record, so the client can see what arrived, what worked, how the data was handled, and where the asset went.

For a healthcare pilot I delivered a verified 130 device test record with device level results and chain of custody documentation from intake to disposition. I then carried those field requirements into the product workflow.

The records

The platform screens below show how the test and custody records are organized. Batch details carry the device list with model, serial, grade, weight, and wipe certificate status. The chain of custody view is a timestamped timeline from pickup request through intake, with the technician, the container seals, and the manifest attached at each step. The evidence view holds the photos and documents that back each record.

The development vendor built these product views from the field workflow, stakeholder roles, and acceptance criteria I led. The wider operating and product story is in the RYGNeco case.
