Following a review into the drawing

A reviewer marked changes to grille tags, return air information, equipment checks, and coordination with the lighting grid. The later sheet shows how those comments feed into a coordinated drawing. This is the kind of detail that has to stay consistent across the plan, notes, and schedules.

Connecting the plan, elevation, and schedule

The plan locates the ductwork and outlets. The elevation explains the relationship to the building. The schedule brings together airflow, required capacity, electrical information, and equipment remarks.
