Project workflow

Architectural plans Trane TRACE load calculations Equipment and airflow schedules AutoCAD and Revit drawings Site review and revisions

I reviewed architectural plans and site information, calculated heating and cooling loads in Trane TRACE, selected and scheduled equipment, and prepared the mechanical information needed by the wider project team.

My drawings and schedules covered HVAC, plumbing, electrical coordination, equipment, and airflow. I worked with architects, engineers, project managers, and contractors, then used site visits to document field conditions and coordinate drawing changes.

Common Revit elements were being recreated from one project to the next. I standardized families, templates, schedules, and repeatable details so new work could begin from a reliable base. That reduced design time by about 25 percent and kept recurring information consistent across the documents.

The drawings belong to MKEC and its clients, so this page shows the verified scope, tools, workflow, and results of my work.
